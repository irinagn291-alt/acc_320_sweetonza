This app requests no permissions. No system permission dialog appears, and there is no usage-description string.

There is no login, no in-app purchase, no ads, no analytics, no user-generated content (no public posts or shared feed; names typed here stay on this device), no account deletion flow, and no App Tracking Transparency prompt.

Spilltile is a tray of daily caps for people who track several limits in one day. Each cap is a named counter on its own page. You tick the counter sitting in the center; while that count is below its brim the tick stays there, and at the brim the next tick spills once into a catch you chose, or drips and the count does not move if the catch is closed.

No credentials or demo account are needed. On a first install on a device, a cold launch shows the system launch screen (dark, no wordmark), then “Loading tray”. A full-screen cover then walks three pages titled “Daily caps”, “Tick until it spills”, and “Peel the last mark”. “Skip” is on every page and closes the cover. “Continue” advances. The last page’s primary button is “Start ticking”. Neither “Skip” nor “Start ticking” adds a counter; both open the tray as it is (empty on a device). After the cover, “Daily caps” shows “Tray empty”, “Add a counter to start a daily cap.”, and “Add a counter”. Tap “Add a counter” to open “Editor”. Enter a “Name”, enter a “Cap” as a whole number of at least 1, and tap “Save counter”. “Save counter” stays dim until “Name” has text. Empty, 0, or non-numeric “Cap” shows “Cap needs a whole number of at least 1.” “Save counter” does not close “Editor”; after a new counter, “Name” and “Cap” clear so another can be added. Use “Close editor” to leave. The line “Add another counter before a catch can open.” stays until a second counter exists. With another counter available, turn “Catch open” on, choose a “Catch” other than “None”, and tap “Save counter” again. A counter cannot catch to itself. Back on “Daily caps”, swipe sideways or tap a name chip so that counter is in the center, then tap “Tick”. The status word is “Flowing” while ticks and spills are moving counts, or “Dripping” after a tick at a closed catch. A tap on “Tick” at brim with “Catch closed, drip.” does not raise the big number. A tap on “Tick” at brim with an open catch does not raise the big number; the “Catch” number goes up, and “Stats” can show that source spilled to that catch. “Hold to peel” is a hold, not a tap; a hold drops the last tick or spill for today, and a hold with no tick or spill today does nothing. Hold does not undo a drip or a ghost. An off-page row does not change any count and raises “Ghosts”. Open “Stats” for “Today” and “Spills”. Open “Settings” to pick a “Counter” and tap “Reset day tally”, or use “Contact support”, “Show onboarding again”, or “Erase tray”. “Show onboarding again” brings the three pages back; counters already on the tray stay. There is no control to delete one counter. If a first run already shows “Calls” (cap 4, today’s count 3, catch “Inbox”), “Inbox” (cap 6), and “Errands” (cap 3), the cover was skipped; the first “Tick” on “Calls” is then a spill into “Inbox”. Later launches skip the cover and reopen the tray with the counters and today’s counts still there.

Everything stays on this device. The erase confirm says “Deletes every counter, mark, and tally on this device. This cannot be undone.” The support control is “Contact support” in “Settings”, under “Contact”, with the line “Opens the support page.”

There is no region lock. The app is English only. Counts use the device’s whole-number format. The day follows the device calendar and time zone.
